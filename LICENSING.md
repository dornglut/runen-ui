# Licensing

RunenUI is currently publicly licensed under `GPL-3.0-only`; see [LICENSE](LICENSE) for the complete public license text.

A separate commercial agreement may be available only from copyright holder(s) with sufficient rights to grant it. That commercial agreement is separate from the public SPDX license and is not represented by the repository's SPDX metadata.

This change is prospective: revisions previously released under MIT retain their already-granted MIT rights. Third-party dependencies and incorporated material remain under their own licenses.

Until reviewed inbound terms preserve commercial relicensing authority, external pull requests contributing tracked repository content, including code, documentation, tests, examples, build scripts, or assets, are not accepted. Issue reports, design discussion, reviews, and reproducible cases may still be accepted when they do not add third-party repository content.
